Four Earned Autonomy Levels (L1–L4)

Chosen per component, not per person. Raised on green evals; dropped automatically on breach.

L1 · Gate Hand-OffHIGH RISK

Agent suggests only; human signs off before next step (e.g. live payment release).

L2 · Human-Led + AssistDEFAULT BUILD

Human drives; coding agent assists on judgement-heavy build tasks.

L3 · Co-WorkDESIGN & SPEC

Real-time pairing for problem framing, UX trust models, and edge cases.

L4 · Bounded AutonomousEARNED

Agent executes inside contract; human approves output (e.g. test generation).

CONTROL 02 Security & Data

Data residency, tenant isolation, secure sandboxes, and least-privilege access.

CONTROL 03 Model & Solution Assurance

Mandatory evaluation harnesses, grounding thresholds, and quality gates before go-live.

CONTROL 04 IP & Commercial

Clear ownership terms, reusable-asset rights in CoE library, and liability guardrails.

The 8 CoE Governance Controls

01 Regulatory Spine: Map use case to EU AI Act, ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF, SR 11-7.
02 Agent Risk Tiering: Tier by business impact and autonomy level at Land.
03 Autonomy & Execution: Enforce L1–L4 limits per component in CI.
04 Lifecycle Mgmt: Version, regression-test, and retire agents on schedule.
05 Human-in-Loop: Mandatory approvals & escalation paths for high-risk actions.
06 Observability: Full OpenTelemetry traces, eval scores & immutable audit trail.
07 Federated Ops: Global CoE sets policy-as-code; pods execute locally.
08 Tool Governance: Allow-listed MCP servers, pinned versions & AIBOM.
